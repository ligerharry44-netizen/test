Since 2019

Made in a converted print shop in Leiden.

We started with one letterpress and a mailing list of forty people. Six years on, the same principle holds: paper should feel worth writing on, and a letter should feel worth sending.

Every notebook is sewn, not glued. Every wax stick is poured by hand. We keep runs small on purpose — it's the only way we've found to keep the quality where we want it.
