Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be completely happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, here's how returns work:

Eligible for return

Unused items in original packaging, within 30 days of delivery. Reach out via the contact page and we'll send you a return label.

Not eligible

Items that have been used, written in, or personalised. Opened ink bottles and used wax sticks cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

Damaged in transit?

If your order arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we'll replace it at no cost — no need to return the original.

Ready to order?

Free shipping on orders over $75. Sealed with wax, shipped with care.
