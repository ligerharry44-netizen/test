Our Story

A print shop, six years, and a lot of very slow ink.

We make the kind of stationery we couldn't find anywhere else — paper that rewards good handwriting, wax that seals cleanly, pens that get better with use.

🏛️

Inkwell & Bond started in 2019 in a converted print shop in Leiden, with one letterpress and a mailing list of forty people. We'd both spent years working in design studios, frustrated by how disposable most paper goods had become.

So we started small: one notebook, sewn by hand, on cotton rag paper we sourced from a mill two towns over. It sold out in a week. We've kept the same instinct ever since — make fewer things, and make them properly.

Today the shop is still small on purpose. Every notebook is still sewn, not glued. Every stick of wax is still poured by hand, in batches you could count on two hands.

How we got here

2019

One letterpress, one notebook

Founded in a converted print shop in Leiden with a single hand-sewn notebook and a mailing list of forty people.

2021

First wax runs

Began casting sealing wax in-house after months of testing formulas that wouldn't crack in transit.

2023

Fountain pens, at last

Partnered with a small brass workshop to produce our first refillable fountain pen, four years after we first sketched it.

2026

Still six people

Same headcount as year two. We'd rather stay small and keep making things by hand than scale past what we can stand behind.

What we hold onto

✂️

Made, not manufactured

Every binding is sewn and every seal is cast by a person, not a machine line. It's slower, and we think it shows.

🌾

Materials first

We choose paper and brass before we choose designs. If the material isn't right, the product doesn't ship.

📮

Built to be used

Nothing we make is precious for its own sake — it's meant to sit on a desk and earn its keep.
